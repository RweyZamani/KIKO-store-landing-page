About KIKO Milano

KIKO Milano is an Italian professional cosmetics brand founded in Milan. The brand is known for offering high-quality makeup and skincare products inspired by the latest beauty trends.

KIKO Milano combines innovation, creativity, and fashion to create products that allow everyone to express their beauty freely and confidently. Today, KIKO Milano stores are present in many countries around the world.

This website is created as a student educational project and is inspired by the original KIKO Milano website. It is not an official or commercial website.
